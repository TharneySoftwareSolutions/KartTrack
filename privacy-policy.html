Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the KartTrack mobile application (the "App"), developed and operated by TharneySoftwareSolutions ("we", "us", or "our"), available on Android (Google Play Store). It describes what information is (and is not) collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Summary

KartTrack does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data you enter in the App (race sessions, kart details, lap times, performance notes, and settings) is stored exclusively on your device using the device's local storage. Nothing is sent to our servers or any third party because we do not operate any server or back-end infrastructure.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, process, or store any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information
  • Location data (GPS or otherwise)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.)
  • IP addresses or network information
  • Cookies or web beacons
  • Analytics or usage statistics
  • Crash reports (no crash-reporting SDK is integrated)
  • Camera, microphone, or file-system data

The App requests no Android permissions beyond those implicitly required to run a standard React Native application.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide its core functionality, KartTrack stores the following data locally on your device only using @react-native-async-storage/async-storage:

  • Race sessions – date, track, conditions, results you enter manually
  • Kart profiles – kart name and descriptive notes you enter manually
  • Performance records – lap times and other metrics you enter manually
  • App settings – language preference and any display options you configure

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to our servers (we have none). It may be included in Android's standard device backup if you have enabled that feature in your device settings; that backup is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not ours.

4. Third-Party Services

KartTrack does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social-login SDKs. The App does not make any network requests to external services.

The only external party involved in distributing the App is Google Play. When you download or use the App through Google Play, Google may collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to that data.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you are in full control:

  • You can delete individual races, karts, or performance records from within the App at any time.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • We have no ability to access or recover your data once deleted.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

KartTrack is a general-audience application and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 for users in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, there is no risk of inadvertent collection from minors. If you believe a child has entered personal data into the App, please uninstall it; all local data is removed upon uninstallation.

7. Your Rights under the GDPR (EEA / UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) grants you certain rights. Because KartTrack does not collect or process any personal data, these rights are satisfied by design (privacy-by-default). Specifically:

  • Right of access – all your data is already visible and accessible directly inside the App.
  • Right to rectification – you can edit any entry in the App at any time.
  • Right to erasure – you can delete individual entries or all data by uninstalling the App.
  • Right to data portability – not applicable; no data is held by us.
  • Right to object / restrict processing – not applicable; we do not process any personal data.

Our legal basis for storing data locally on your device is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): providing the core functionality of the App requires persisting the data you explicitly enter. No profiling or automated decision-making is performed.

8. California Privacy Rights (CalOPPA & CCPA)

Under the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have specific rights regarding personal information. Because KartTrack does not collect, sell, share, or disclose any personal information, the following applies:

  • We do not sell personal information to third parties.
  • We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • There is no personal information held by us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf.
  • We do not discriminate against users who exercise their privacy rights.

If you have questions about our data practices under California law, you may contact us at the address below.

9. Security

Since no personal data is transmitted over a network, there is no risk of interception. Data stored locally is protected by the security mechanisms of your Android device (device encryption, screen lock, etc.). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
